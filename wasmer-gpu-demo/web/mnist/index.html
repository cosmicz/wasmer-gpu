    What this proves, exactly

    The trainer is a Wasmer WASIX guest. It owns the parameters on the GPU and drives every minibatch: it writes the batch into its buffer and dispatches its own kernels through wasmer_gpu_v0: conv 5x5 with 8 filters and ReLU, 2x2 max-pool, a fully connected layer to 10 classes with softmax, and the matching gradient and SGD kernels. The host feeds raw MNIST bytes and reads numbers back. Train split: first 12,000 official training images; held-out: first 2,000 official test images, never trained on.

    Model: reported by the trainer when a run starts.

    Not here: a second conv layer, shuffling, data augmentation, or any speedup claim. Timings are wall-clock on a small model.
